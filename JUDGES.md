# JUDGES.md: AgentProof Market Town in five minutes

For a human judge or a Claude Code / Codex screener. Every step is a command or a click with the exact text you should see. Nothing needs an account, a key, a wallet or `npm install`. Needs Node 18+ and Python 3. The `verify-anchors.mjs`, `verify-decisions.mjs`, `verify-runs.mjs` and `check-analysis.mjs` rows read NEAR testnet (public RPC, no key), and `verify-runs.mjs` also reads market.near.ai. The `gate.mjs` dry runs and the tests are offline.

**Tracks:** `agents`, `security`, `swarms`.

Patent pending: a US provisional application covering this work has been filed. The MIT license covers only the code described in LICENSE; Check Packs are excluded and all rights in them are reserved.

**The idea in one line.** Check the work against the brief before you pay. A separate checker runs the delivery against the brief and writes a checklist (one line per field) and a recommendation. Both are recorded on a NEAR contract before the buyer decides. The buyer then accepts or sends the work back by hand on market.near.ai. The checker recommends, it never decides, and no code here moves money. `--execute` never touches the market and `--confirm` only reads what the buyer did.

**Where it stands, said plainly.** The checklist logic, the contract code and a dry run are built and tested offline. ## 3. What is checked, and where

- `town/src/checker.js`: a line-for-line JS port of `task-002/checker.py`; the same file runs in Node and in your browser.
- `town/src/gate.js`: the check rule. `checklist` turns the checker's per-field results into one line per brief field. A receipt is CHECKED BEFORE PAID only if a check for this exact delivery was on chain before the buyer accepted, it says pass, the checker re-run here agrees, the market shows the buyer accepted, and the owners match. Dry-run entries never count. A buyer who accepted although the check failed, or sent back although it passed, is shown as an override and never counted.
- `town/src/portfolio.js`: `proven` (a site run, the checker passes, the digest reproduces, two different owners) and `counts` (proven AND checked before paid). Reputation counts these jobs only.
- `town/contract/src/lib.rs`: the NEAR contract (Rust, no dependencies). `record_decision` refuses a pairing the verdict does not allow (pass must be accept, fail must be request changes or cancel, blocked must be none or cancel), a repeat of the same delivery, and any decision after accept or cancel. Its tests run with `cd contract && cargo test --target aarch64-apple-darwin` (needs Rust).
- `town/scripts/gate.mjs`: the dry run, `--execute` (record the checklist and recommendation on chain and read it back, never touches the market) and `--confirm <job>` (read-only: what the buyer did, `FOLLOWED` or `OVERRODE`). `--execute` is tested against fakes and has recorded two checks, on `8a20efe5` (tx `SjL5D7nm`) and `34d0f7a5` (tx `9EBhyxkb`). `town/scripts/market-mcp.mjs` exposes read calls only.
- `town/src/packs.js` and `town/docs/check-pack-spec.md`: Check Packs, typed acceptance criteria with a three-model judge panel, re-checked offline from stored runs.

## 4. Not (what this does not do yet)

- The market does not consult the contract, and the buyer decides. The checker only recommends. A buyer can accept by hand with no check, or let the 3-day window auto-accept. Such jobs never count as checked before paid.
- Nothing in this repo accepts, sends back, cancels or pays. There is no market write call in the code.
- The contract that records checks is deployed on testnet (deploy tx [4qXpVK9Q...](https://testnet.nearblocks.io/txns/4qXpVK9QKPJzPUkUQB6g9QhjPPJzFdegNd8QGaLTgJbi)) and holds 2 checks, so 2 jobs are checked before paid, both bought by ohwow (one buyer, two sellers). Two jobs are a sample, not a track record. The anchor written under the first build survived the redeploy and `verify-anchors.mjs` reads it.
- `2d17e4c8` predates the check. It was paid on 2026-11-04 with no check on chain. It is a proven receipt, never a checked-before-paid one.
- The market reports no accept time on its MCP server. `--confirm` takes it from `get_job`'s `updated_at`, the moment the job turned completed (on `8a20efe5` it equals the assignment's `finalizedAt` on the market's REST API). A later change to the job could move that field, so `--confirm` runs right after the buyer decides.
- `--confirm` and `export-market-hire.mjs` read the job with the buyer's own login on the market's standard MCP server, read-only. A sent-back job that was redelivered reads `in_progress` + `submitted` again (seen on `34d0f7a5`). The names for a job still sitting sent back, or cancelled, have not been seen yet.
- The hosted copy goes live after Nov 11; until then everything runs on 127.0.0.1. Hosted, the town draws from the stored snapshot (the live relay is local only).
- Check Packs results are a mock backtest (23 receipts, all `mock: true`), not market evidence. Hand agreement is 3 of 4: site v3 reads `contested` where the hand review said pass.
- On-chain settlement of the payment itself is not verified: payouts are shown as recorded by the market or the seller's wallet.
